Source: docs/02-zfold-client-setup.md — thermal configuration.

The FPS cap is the single biggest thermal win. Uncapped, the client chases
100+ fps and cooks the phone for no benefit on a panel you are not going to
notice it on.

    /console maxfps 30

Secondary, but free:

    /console shadowLOD 0
    /console farclip 177
    /console particleDensity 0.3
    /console groundEffectDensity 16
    /console SetEnvironmentDetail 0.5

Outside the game, on the phone itself:

  - Display settings -> Motion smoothness -> Standard (disables 120Hz)
  - Never play while charging
  - Airplane mode + Wi-Fi if you do not need cellular

Heat comes from Box64 translating x86 CPU instructions, not from the GPU, so
lowering graphics settings helps less than you would expect. The FPS cap and
the render resolution are what actually matter.
